📖 How to Play

  1. TV screen shows the garden for everyone to see.
  2. Players join on their phones by entering the room code.
  3. Each player gets a different role with special abilities.
  4. Don't show your phone screen to others!
  5. Talk to each other — describe your clues and work as a team.
  6. Each round lasts 3 minutes. There are 5 rounds.
  7. Tap the right plots on your phone to solve garden problems.
  8. If the garden is healthy after 5 rounds, your team wins!
